# TaxsPoint — India's Premier LegalTech & Tax Filing Platform

> Exact replica and enhanced legaltech portal inspired by RegisterKaro, branded exclusively for **TaxsPoint** with the tagline **"INCOME TAX FILING | LEGAL CONSULTING"**.

![TaxsPoint Logo](/taxspoint-logo.png)

## 🌟 Key Highlights & Feature Matrix

1. **Brand Identity**:
   - Built using the custom TaxsPoint logo (Scales of Justice + Growth Chart + "TaxsPoint — INCOME TAX FILING | LEGAL CONSULTING").
   - Deep navy and royal blue palette (`#062a4d`, `#0062cc`, `#16a34a`) matching the official identity.

2. **5 Alternative AI Specialists (Multi-Agent Advisory Center)**:
   - **CA Rajesh Sharma (`@taxbot`)**: Direct Tax & ITR Specialist (Old vs New Regime comparisons, Sec 44ADA presumptive taxation, capital gains post-Budget 2024, Section 143(1) intimation notices).
   - **Adv. Priya Nair (`@corpbot`)**: Corporate Law & MCA Incorporation Specialist (SPICe+ Part A & B, Pvt Ltd vs LLP structuring, NRI/Foreign directors, MCA Rule 8 name clearance).
   - **Vikram Singhania (`@gstbot`)**: Indirect Tax & GST Specialist (GST thresholds, GSTR-1 & 3B compliance, GSTR-2B ITC reconciliation, Export LUT Form RFD-11, DRC-01B variance notices).
   - **Adv. Sneha Mukherjee (`@ipbot`)**: Trademark & Intellectual Property Attorney (NICE Classification Classes 1 to 45, Section 9 & 11 objection replies, software copyright, ™ vs ® guidelines).
   - **Rohan Mehta (`@startupbot`)**: Startup Legal & Venture Counsel (Founders' Agreements with 4-year reverse vesting & 1-year cliff, DPIIT recognition, 3-year Section 80-IAC tax holiday, i-SAFE notes, ESOP pool).

3. **Complete Service Catalog & Pricing**:
   - **Company Registration**: Private Limited Company (from ₹1,999), LLP (from ₹1,499), One Person Company (from ₹1,999), Section 8 NGO (from ₹4,999).
   - **Tax & Compliance**: GST Registration (from ₹499), GST Monthly Return Filing (from ₹499/mo), Income Tax Return Filing (from ₹799), Annual RoC Compliance (from ₹4,999).
   - **Trademark & IP**: Trademark Registration (from ₹1,499), Trademark Objection Reply (from ₹1,999), Copyright Registration (from ₹2,999).
   - **Licenses & Approvals**: MSME / Udyam (from ₹499), FSSAI Food License (from ₹999), Import Export Code (IEC) (from ₹999), Startup India DPIIT (from ₹1,999).

4. **Multi-Step Online Application Flow & Checkout**:
   - Step 1: Applicant Contact Information
   - Step 2: Proposed Entity Name & KYC Document Upload Simulation
   - Step 3: Transparent Fee Summary (Professional Fee + 18% GST) & Order Submission with celebratory confetti!

5. **Client Portal & Live Filing Tracker**:
   - Real-time 5-stage progress stepper:
     1. Application & Payment Confirmed
     2. KYC & Address Verification
     3. DSC Generation & RUN Name Approval
     4. Govt Portal Filing (MCA / GSTN / TMR)
     5. Approved Certificate Issued & Downloadable
   - Secure 256-bit encrypted **Document Vault** to view PAN, Aadhaar, and download official Certificates.
   - Assigned Senior CA details with direct call & message triggers.

6. **Executive CRM & Admin Operations Hub**:
   - Manage incoming consultation leads and callbacks with one-click status updates ("Converted", "Callback Scheduled").
   - Live Order Management: update filing stages (0 to 4), track total revenue processed, and view CA utilization metrics.

7. **Free Financial & MCA Calculators**:
   - **Income Tax Calculator (FY 2024-25 / AY 2025-26)**: Side-by-side comparison of Old vs New Tax Regimes, calculating Standard Deduction (₹75k vs ₹50k), Section 87A rebate, 80C/80D/HRA, and tax difference savings.
   - **GST Calculator**: Inclusive and Exclusive GST computations for 5%, 12%, 18%, 28% slabs.
   - **MCA Company Name Availability Simulator**: Checks proposed names against MCA Rule 8 guidelines and restricted keywords.

## 🚀 Running the Project

The application is running live on:
**http://localhost:3000/**

To run manually or restart at any time:
```bash
cd C:\Taxspoint
npm run dev -- --port 3000
```

To build for production:
```bash
npm run build
```
Production output is created inside `C:\Taxspoint\dist`.
